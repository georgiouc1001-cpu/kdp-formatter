Turn your manuscript into a KDP-ready interior

Upload a Word file or plain text. You get a print PDF with the right trim size, gutter margins, chapter openings and page numbers. Your file never leaves your browser.

Headings are found from Word Heading styles, lines starting with “#”, and lines such as “Chapter 3” or “Prologue”. Scene breaks are lines of “***”, “* * *” or “#”. This version sets plain text: italics and bold from your manuscript are not carried over yet.
