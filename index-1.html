Turn your manuscript into a KDP-ready interior

Upload a Word file or plain text. You get a print PDF with the right trim size, gutter margins, chapter openings, italics, embedded fonts and page numbers. Your manuscript never leaves your browser.

Headings are found from Word Heading styles, lines starting with “#”, and lines such as “Chapter 3” or “Prologue”. Scene breaks are lines of “***”, “* * *” or “#”. Italics and bold are kept. The first time you format, the book font downloads (a few seconds).
